Mohamed Amine Hebilez

What he does

He builds websites, edits videos, runs social media ads and designs clean UX/UI — helping brands grow in the digital world.

He has more than five years of hands-on video editing experience (Reels, TikToks, YouTube videos and video ads), and works on website development with HTML5, CSS and Bootstrap, UX/UI and PSD design, and social media advertising campaigns.

NOVA AGENCY

He founded NOVA AGENCY, a digital agency in Algeria whose slogan is “Building Digital Experiences. Empowering Businesses.” The agency works on website development, UI/UX design, branding, digital solutions, video editing and social media advertising.
